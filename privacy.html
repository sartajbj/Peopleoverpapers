YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

People Over Papers operates a community information map and educational resource website. This policy explains, in plain language, what information may be handled when you visit the site or submit a report. The map is not a government service, emergency alert system, law firm or legal advice service.

Information you choose to submit

If you submit a community report, the form may collect the report category, a broad city or area, an approximate map location, the date and time you observed something, and the description you provide. An optional image may also be submitted. Reports are reviewed before publication. Approved reports may be displayed with a generalized location; pending and rejected reports are not displayed publicly.

Please do not submit names, faces, exact home addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, license plates, immigration status, private documents or other information that identifies or could endanger a person. Do not upload photographs of people, vehicles, enforcement encounters or private records. Optional images should be limited to non-identifying public resource notices, legal-help flyers or community information signs.

How submitted information is used

We use submitted information to review reports, operate the community map, prevent misuse, respond to safety or privacy concerns, and maintain the website. Moderators may edit, decline or remove submissions that are unsafe, identifying, misleading, unlawful or outside the site's purpose. A report is a community submission, not independently verified fact.

Photos and location privacy

Photos are optional. The site processes selected images before submission and makes them available for moderator review. A photo is not shown publicly unless its report is approved. Locations shown on the map are approximate rather than exact. These safeguards reduce privacy risks, but no online service can guarantee complete anonymity or eliminate every possibility of identification.

Technical information and hosting

Like most websites, our hosting and security services may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the site. This may include IP address, browser and device information, requested pages, timestamps and security-related logs. The site uses Cloudflare Pages and its database services to host the website and handle submitted reports. Service providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

Some site features or service providers may use essential storage or similar technologies to operate, protect or improve the website. If analytics, advertising or embedded third-party services are enabled, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies and receive technical information in accordance with their own policies. We will update this policy when material changes are made to the site's use of these services. Visitors can also manage cookies through their browser settings.

External websites

Our resources may link to government agencies, nonprofit organizations, map services or other external websites. We do not control those websites. Review their privacy policies before sharing personal information with them.

Data retention and your requests

We keep submitted reports and related information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the map, maintain moderation records, address abuse or safety issues, and meet applicable obligations. Retention periods may vary. You may contact us to ask about a report you submitted or to request a correction or removal. To help us locate it, include the approximate submission date and report details, but do not email sensitive personal documents.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not submit information about a child or upload an image that identifies a child.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website changes. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates when the current version was published.

Contact us about privacy

For privacy questions, correction requests or removal requests, email peopleoverpapersmap@gmail.com or use our Contact page.

This policy is general website information, not legal advice. It should be reviewed and updated if the site's features, service providers or legal obligations change.
