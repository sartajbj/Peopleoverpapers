YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice

Last updated: November 3, 2026

People Over Papers is an independent community information project. This notice explains the intended handling of information submitted through this website. It must be reviewed and finalized by the site operator before public launch.

Information you submit

The report form requests a general location, report category, description and date/time. Coordinates, if supplied, are rounded before storage to reduce location precision. Submissions are stored as pending and are not displayed publicly unless approved through a separate moderation process.

Public information

Only approved entries are intended to appear on the public map. Do not submit names, faces, license plates, private home addresses, immigration identifiers, or other sensitive personal information. The project should remove identifying details before publication.

Service providers and logs

The site may be hosted by Cloudflare and use OpenStreetMap map services and Nominatim location search. Those providers may process technical request data under their own terms and privacy notices. Hosting logs and retention settings must be reviewed by the operator.

Retention and requests

The operator must establish a retention schedule, a contact channel for correction or deletion requests, and a secure moderation process before accepting real submissions.

Contact

The site operator must publish a monitored contact address and a process for privacy, correction and deletion requests before collecting public submissions. No contact address has been confirmed yet.

This is a starter privacy notice, not legal advice. Obtain a jurisdiction-specific privacy review before collecting real user submissions.
