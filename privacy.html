YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy notice

Last updated: November 3, 2026

PeopleOverPapersMap.com is an independent informational guide. It does not operate the People Over Papers community map, collect sightings, accept report submissions, host user accounts or provide legal services.

Information handled by this website

The map submission form asks for a category, city and state, approximate map coordinates, the date an observation occurred and a short description. Do not submit names, faces, phone numbers, email addresses, exact addresses, license plates, immigration status or other identifying or sensitive information. Submissions are stored privately in a Cloudflare D1 database while pending review. Only approved reports are displayed publicly, with coordinates generalized to roughly 10–11 km and publication delayed at least 24 hours. The form does not request an account or contact details.

Hosting and technical logs

The website is intended to be hosted on Cloudflare Pages. The hosting provider and its infrastructure may process technical information such as IP addresses, request timestamps, browser details and security logs to deliver and protect the website. The operator should review the hosting provider's current privacy documentation and configure available retention controls. Pending and approved report records are retained in the site database until removed by the site operator.

External links

This guide may link to government legal-resource directories, nonprofit organizations and OpenStreetMap map tiles/geocoding. When you follow an external link, that provider's privacy practices apply. We do not control their websites or data handling.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This version of the website does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies. The map uses OpenStreetMap tiles and a place-search service; requests to those providers may disclose your IP address and the map area being requested. If analytics, advertising, embedded content or other tracking technologies are added later, this notice must be updated before they are enabled and any legally required consent must be obtained.

Privacy questions and corrections

For privacy questions or correction requests, contact us at peopleoverpapersmap@gmail.com. We aim to review genuine requests and respond when reasonably possible.

This notice is a plain-language starting point, not legal advice. The operator must verify it against the actual hosting configuration, jurisdiction and any future third-party services before launch.
