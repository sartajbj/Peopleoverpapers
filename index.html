KNOW YOUR RIGHTS

Stay informed.
Make a plan.

Immigration law and enforcement procedures can be complex and vary by circumstance. General information is not a substitute for advice from a licensed attorney or accredited representative.

  • You can ask to speak with a qualified legal representative.
  • Do not sign documents you do not understand.
  • Keep important contact information somewhere safe.
  • For a specific case, seek individualized legal advice.

This site does not provide legal advice or create an attorney-client relationship.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Are map reports verified?

Submissions are reviewed before publication, but review does not guarantee every detail is independently verified. Check report dates and status; a map entry is not an official alert.

Why are exact locations not shown?

We aim to reduce the risk of exposing private individuals. Public map locations should be approximate and must never identify a private home, person or sensitive location.

Can I submit a photo or video?

The initial reporting form accepts text and approximate location only. Do not send images that identify people, show license plates or reveal private addresses.

Does this website provide legal advice?

No. It provides general educational information and community resources. For advice about a particular case, contact a licensed attorney or accredited representative.

What if a report is wrong?

Do not amplify information you believe may be inaccurate. Contact the project team with the report details so it can be reviewed, corrected or removed.
