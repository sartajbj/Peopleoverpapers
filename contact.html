Privacy and safety

Please do not email passwords, identity documents, immigration records, private addresses or other sensitive personal information. If you are asking us to review a report, share its approximate submission date and general description only.

Important to know

People Over Papers is a community information project, not an emergency service, law firm or legal representative. We cannot provide individual legal advice or guarantee a response to urgent matters. For emergencies, contact appropriate local emergency services. For immigration legal advice, consult a qualified attorney or DOJ-accredited representative.
