Informational purpose only

Website content and community submissions are general information, not legal advice, a legal opinion, an official alert or a guarantee of current conditions. Laws and procedures vary. Consult a licensed attorney or accredited representative about an individual matter.

Community reports

Submissions may be delayed, incomplete, mistaken or unverified. Review before publication does not guarantee accuracy. Do not rely on a map entry as the sole basis for a safety, legal or travel decision.

Prohibited submissions

Do not submit threats, harassment, private personal information, exact private addresses, identifying images, license plates, unsubstantiated accusations, real-time tactical details, or content intended to locate, track, target or endanger a person. The operator may reject, edit for privacy, or remove submissions.

Emergencies

This website is not monitored as an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency service directly. In the United States, call 911 for immediate emergencies.
