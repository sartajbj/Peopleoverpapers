What this website provides

People Over Papers is an independent community information project. It provides a map of moderated community submissions and links to general educational and support resources. It is not a government agency, law firm, emergency service, legal representative or official alert system. We are not affiliated with any government agency unless a page expressly says otherwise.

Reports are not proof or live alerts

Reports are submitted by members of the public and may be delayed, incomplete, duplicated, inaccurate or outdated. Moderation is intended to screen submissions for suitability and privacy; it does not mean that every statement has been independently verified. Map locations are approximate. Do not rely on a report as proof, a real-time update or the sole basis for a safety, legal, travel or other important decision.

Not legal advice or emergency assistance

Information on this website is for general educational purposes only. It does not create an attorney-client relationship and is not a substitute for advice from a qualified immigration attorney or DOJ-accredited representative. For an immediate emergency, contact appropriate local emergency services. Do not use this map to make urgent safety decisions.

Rules for submitting reports

Submit only information you believe is relevant to the site's community-information purpose. Use a broad area and approximate location. Do not include anyone's name, face, exact home address, phone number, email address, license plate, immigration status or other identifying or sensitive details. Do not upload images of people, vehicles, enforcement encounters, private documents or identifying locations. Optional photos should show only non-identifying public resource notices, legal-help flyers or community information signs.

Moderation and removal

Reports are reviewed before they appear publicly. We may edit for clarity or privacy, decline, hide or remove any submission at our discretion, including content that appears identifying, unsafe, abusive, unlawful, misleading, irrelevant or outside the site's purpose. We may restrict access or submissions to protect the website and its visitors.
