Community map and information

PeopleOverPapersMap.com is a community information website with moderated report submissions. This website provides general information, a community-submission form and educational resources. Submissions are not published automatically. Reports are reviewed, generalized and delayed; this is not a live incident tracker or emergency service.

Informational purpose only

Content is provided for general educational and navigational purposes. It is not legal advice, a legal opinion, an official alert, an emergency service or a guarantee of current conditions. Immigration law and individual circumstances vary. Consult a licensed attorney or DOJ-accredited representative about a specific matter.

Community map and third-party links

The map uses OpenStreetMap tiles and place search. Links to government websites and nonprofit directories may be provided as additional references. We do not control third-party services, their availability, content, privacy practices or decisions.

Accuracy and limitations

We make reasonable efforts to keep this guide useful, but external websites and service details can change. A community-sourced report may be delayed, incomplete, duplicated or mistaken. Do not rely on a map pin as proof of current activity or as the sole basis for a safety, legal or travel decision.

Safe and lawful use

Do not use this guide or linked services to harass, threaten, identify, follow, track, target or endanger any person. Do not submit names, faces, private addresses, license plates, phone numbers, email addresses, immigration status or identifying details. Optional images are limited to non-identifying public resource notices, legal-help flyers and community information signs; do not upload people, vehicles, enforcement scenes, faces, plates, private documents or addresses. Images are resized and re-encoded as WebP in the browser, stored in D1, remain private until a moderator inspects and approves them, and approved images are published only after the report delay. Uploads are limited to 100 KB. We may reject or remove unsafe, identifying, inaccurate or unlawful submissions.
